**Crown of Embers** is an original turn-based card duel: two duelists with 60 life each fight for the Ember Crown with a 36-card deck (ranks 6–A in four houses). Every card strikes for its rank, and its house adds a twist — Spades pierce for +2 damage, Hearts mend half the attack, Clubs rally a fresh card, Diamonds raise a ward that blocks damage. Spend refilling energy to play cards by mouse or keyboard (1–7), outplay the AI Rival, and take the crown.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Open game/index.html in a desktop browser to play.
