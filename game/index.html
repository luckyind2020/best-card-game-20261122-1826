♥ Crown of Embers ♠

A turn-based card duel for the Ember Crown

Two duelists, sixty life each, one crown. Spend energy to play cards from your hand — every card strikes, and its house adds a twist: Spades pierce deeper, Hearts mend your wounds, Clubs rally a fresh card, Diamonds raise a ward.

  • 🃏 Each turn you draw a card and your energy refills.
  • ⚡ A card costs energy equal to its pips (1–3). Attack equals its rank (6–14).
  • ♥ Mend heals half the attack · ♠ Pierce deals +2 · ♣ Rally draws 1 · ♦ Ward blocks damage.
  • 🏆 Reduce the Rival to 0 life to take the crown.

How to play

  • Goal: bring the Rival's life from 60 to 0 before yours falls.
  • Your turn: you draw 1 card and your energy refills (2 at first, up to 5).
  • Play a card: click it, or press keys 1–7. It costs the gold pips shown; its attack number is the damage it deals.
  • Houses: ♠ Spades Pierce (+2 damage) · ♥ Hearts Mend (heal half the attack) · ♣ Clubs Rally (draw 1 card) · ♦ Diamonds Ward (gain a shield that blocks damage).
  • Shields absorb damage first, then break.
  • End turn: press the End turn button, Space, or Enter. The Rival then plays.
  • Deck: 36 cards (ranks 6–A in four houses). When it runs out, your discard pile is reshuffled.
